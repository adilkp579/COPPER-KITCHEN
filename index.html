Our Kitchens Across Malabar

At Copper Kitchen, our chefs are more than just cooks — they are the keepers of a culinary legacy built on authentic Malabar spices, time-honoured techniques, and a deep love for Kerala's food traditions. Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries the fingerprints of hands that have spent decades perfecting the art of slow cooking, and the heart of a team that treats every guest like family. From the vibrant streets of Kozhikode to the bustling kitchens of our Thamarassery flagship and the misty hills of Wayanad, each Copper Kitchen location is led by culinary masters who bring their own regional expertise — biriyani specialists, dessert artisans, grill masters, and seafood experts — all united by one mission: to serve you the finest flavours of Kerala with warmth, care, and consistency. Explore the chefs and the team behind each of our locations below, and discover the people who make Copper Kitchen a place worth returning to.

Our Story

From a Small Kitchen to Thamarassery's Favourite

A journey of passion, tradition and the warmth of Kerala hospitality — told in four chapters.

01

A Humble Beginning

What began as a small family kitchen in the heart of Thamarassery, with a single stove, a handful of recipes, and an unwavering dream.

02

The Signature Taste

Our legendary Manthi and Biriyani were born — crafted with authentic Malabar spices, slow-cooked tradition, and a secret passed down through generations.

03

A Growing Family

Generations of families, travellers and food lovers made Copper Kitchen their own. Every plate served became a memory shared, every guest a friend.

04

Thamarassery's Favourite

Today, we stand proud as one of Thamarassery's most beloved dining destinations — still cooking with the same love, the same spices, and the same heart.

Our Story

The Kozhikode Chapter of Copper Kitchen

From the legendary food streets of Kozhikode to our kitchen — a story of tradition, taste, and the city that loves to eat.

01

The City of Flavours

Kozhikode — the land of the legendary biriyani, the famous halwa, and a food culture that runs deep in every street and every home. It was only natural that Copper Kitchen would find its second home here.

02

Bringing Our Legacy

We carried with us the same recipes, the same spices, and the same heart that made Copper Kitchen a household name in Thamarassery. Every dish, prepared exactly the way it always has been.

03

Embracing Kozhikode

Kozhikode's food lovers welcomed us with warmth. We added local favourites to our menu — authentic Kozhikode biriyani, real halwa, fresh seafood grills — alongside our signature Manthi and Biriyani.

04

A Kozhikode Favourite

Today, Copper Kitchen Kozhikode stands proud as one of the city's most loved dining destinations — a place where tradition meets taste, and every guest leaves with a smile.

Our Story

Copper Kitchen in the Hills of Wayanad

Where Kerala's hill country meets Malabar's soul — a story written among misty mountains, spice gardens, and warm hearts.

01

Into the Hills

We brought Copper Kitchen to Wayanad — a land of misty mountains, lush spice gardens, and warm-hearted people who believe that good food is the best way to bring people together.

02

Tribal & Forest Flavours

Wayanad's tribal cuisine is a treasure — wild mushrooms, bamboo-cooked rice, forest spices, and organic honey. We embraced these ancient flavours and made them part of our kitchen.

03

From Forest to Table

Every ingredient matters. We source fresh produce directly from local Wayanad farms, spice gardens, and forest collectors — bringing the true essence of the hills to your plate.

04

A Hill Country Favourite

Today, Copper Kitchen Wayanad is fast becoming a favourite among locals and travellers alike — a place where great food, cool mountain air, and warm hospitality come together.
